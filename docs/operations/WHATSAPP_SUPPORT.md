# WhatsApp support — JP-WA-001

Official WhatsApp number: **+216 29 302 202** (`21629302202` for `wa.me`). The number is an approved business identity value and is not editable through routine settings. Administrators may edit response hours and expectation wording.

Product links prefill only product name and canonical product URL. They never transmit the message until the customer explicitly sends it in WhatsApp. No customer message content is stored by Jouvence Para Core.

On the cart page, the customer may open a WhatsApp draft containing at most ten
currently visible cart products, their quantities, and their public product
links. Hidden, deleted or invalid products are omitted; an omission count is
shown in the draft when applicable. The message asks the shop to confirm price
and availability. No contact/address details, order data, cart/session token,
prices, or customer-entered message is included in the link. The draft is sent
only if the customer submits it in WhatsApp. Cart contents are read from the
current WooCommerce cart at page render; a product that changes after render
must be rechecked by the customer and shop before an order is placed.
