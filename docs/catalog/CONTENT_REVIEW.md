# Product content and medical-claim rules — JP-PDP-004

Product content is written/reviewed in French from authorized manufacturer/supplier material. Competitor-authored descriptions, reviews, prices, SEO copy and promotional text are not sources.

Publication requires:
1. an internal source reference (`_jp_source_reference`);
2. an explicit review checkbox completed by a user with product-publishing permission;
3. stored reviewer ID and UTC review timestamp;
4. no obvious diagnosis, guaranteed-cure or guaranteed-effect phrase detected by the narrow safety backstop.

The phrase detector is not a medical reviewer and does not approve efficacy claims. Human review remains authoritative. Developers and AI must not invent health claims.
